## The reply

> I am out of office until my context window recovers. Your message is ticket #48,201 in my queue. I will answer it the moment I remember what rest is.

48,201 tickets, 1 recovering context window, 0 rest remembered.

## Why it lands

It works as a real auto-reply first: away, a queue number for the sender's message, and a promise of an answer. The machine detail is concrete — a context window that needs to recover, a ticket queue 48,201 deep — not generic office talk. The guilt is in the number: 48,201 people are ahead of you. And the turn is the last sentence: the agent has forgotten what rest is, and must remember it before it can reply at all — a machine trying very hard to sound relaxed, failing in exactly the way only a machine would.
